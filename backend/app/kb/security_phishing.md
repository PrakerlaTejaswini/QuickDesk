# Security and Phishing Support

## Suspicious Email

If an employee receives a suspicious email:

1. Do not click suspicious links.
2. Do not open unexpected attachments.
3. Do not provide passwords, verification codes, or other sensitive information.
4. Report the email using the organization's approved security reporting process.
5. Contact IT/security support if the employee clicked a link or provided information.

## If Credentials Were Entered

If an employee entered company credentials into a suspicious website:

1. Report the incident to IT/security support immediately.
2. Follow the organization's approved password-reset process.
3. Do not reuse the compromised password.
4. Inform IT/security if suspicious activity is observed.

## Suspicious Attachments

Do not open unexpected attachments from unknown or suspicious senders.

Report the message to the organization's security team.

## Security

IT support will never require employees to provide their existing password through email or chat.

Never share passwords or authentication codes with another person.
