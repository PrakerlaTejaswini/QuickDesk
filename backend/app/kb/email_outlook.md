# Email / Outlook Support

## Outlook is Not Sending Emails

If an employee cannot send emails through Outlook:

1. Check that the computer is connected to the internet.
2. Verify that Outlook is connected to the company account.
3. Check whether messages are stuck in the Outbox.
4. Restart Outlook and try sending the email again.
5. Verify that the recipient's email address is correct.
6. If the problem continues, contact IT support.

## Outlook is Not Receiving Emails

If new emails are not appearing:

1. Check the internet connection.
2. Restart Outlook.
3. Use the Send/Receive option.
4. Check whether the mailbox has reached its storage limit.
5. Check the Junk Email folder.
6. Contact IT support if the problem continues.

## Security

Do not share your company password through email or chat.

Do not open suspicious attachments or links.

If an email appears suspicious, report it to IT/security support.
