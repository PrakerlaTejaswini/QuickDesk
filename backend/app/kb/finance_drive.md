# Finance Drive Access Troubleshooting

## Finance Drive Access Denied

If an employee cannot access the company Finance shared drive, follow these steps:

1. Verify that the employee is connected to the company network or VPN.

2. Confirm that the employee is using their company account.

3. Check whether the employee has been granted permission to access the Finance shared drive.

4. If the employee recently changed teams or joined the Finance department, verify that the appropriate access group has been assigned.

5. Ask the employee to sign out and sign back into their company account.

6. If access is still denied, IT support should verify the employee's permissions in the company's access management system.

## Security

Do not provide or share Finance Drive credentials.

Do not bypass access controls.

Only authorized IT administrators should change Finance Drive permissions.

## Required Information

When escalating the issue, collect:

- Employee name
- Employee email
- Exact error message
- Approximate time the problem occurred
- Whether the employee can access other shared drives
