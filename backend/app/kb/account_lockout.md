# Company Account Lockout

## Account is Locked

If an employee's company account is locked:

1. Stop repeatedly entering the password.
2. Verify that the employee is using the correct company username.
3. Wait for the organization's approved account unlock period, if applicable.
4. Use the approved password reset or account recovery process.
5. If self-service recovery is unavailable, contact IT support.
6. IT support can verify the employee's identity and assist with account recovery.

## Security

Do not share passwords with IT support or other employees.

IT support should follow the organization's identity verification process before unlocking an account.

Do not attempt to bypass account security controls.
