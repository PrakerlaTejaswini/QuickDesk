# Expense Reimbursement

Employees should submit business expense reimbursement requests through the approved expense management system.

Each request should include the expense date, amount, business purpose, and required receipt or supporting document.

Employees should submit reimbursement requests according to the company's submission deadline.

If a reimbursement is rejected or delayed, the employee should check the expense system for comments and contact Finance for clarification.

Support agents should not manually approve financial reimbursements.
